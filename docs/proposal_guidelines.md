# Project Proposal Guidelines

**Due:** Friday 16 October 2026. One submission per group. Ungraded, but required, and you will receive written feedback.

## What to include

One page is enough. State:

- **The question** you are asking, in one sentence.
- **The dataset**, with a link. It must be public and already usable; do not plan to collect or scrape your own. Name its licence or terms of use and check they permit what you intend, including redistribution of figures derived from it. Many popular datasets do not.
- **The method** you expect to use, and which course module it comes from.
- **What a baseline and a result would look like.**
- **Group members** and how you plan to divide the work.

## Purpose

The purpose is to catch projects that cannot work before you have invested in them. A vague proposal gets vague feedback.

Novelty is not required. A careful comparison of methods from the course on a public dataset is a full-credit project. Propose something you can actually finish by 11 December.

## Data restrictions

Do not use datasets containing personal or sensitive data about identifiable individuals: health records, biometrics, private communications and the like. Aggregated or properly de-identified public data is fine. If you are unsure whether a dataset qualifies, ask before you build on it.
